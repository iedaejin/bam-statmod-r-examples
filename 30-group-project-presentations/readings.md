## Before the session

- Upload your slides (PDF) to Blackboard before the session starts. File name: `slotN-groupname.pdf`.
- Rehearse with a timer: 10 minutes, every member speaks.
- Check that the last slide has the data source and the GenAI disclosure statement.
- Prepare one question for the group that presents after you (the last group asks the first).

## Exercises (for the audience)

1. For each talk, write down the research question in one sentence. If you cannot, that is your feedback.
2. For each talk, identify the model comparison and say whether it is valid (same response, same rows, same likelihood).
3. After the session, choose the best figure you saw and explain in two sentences why it worked.
