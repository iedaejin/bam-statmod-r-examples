# Session 12 — Readings

**Topic:** GLM exercises
**Faculty:** Prof. Dae-Jin Lee (`daelee@faculty.ie.edu`)

## Required

| Text | Sections | Focus |
|------|----------|-------|
| Wood (2017), *Generalized Additive Models: An Introduction with R*, 2nd ed. | Ch. 3 exercises (those on exponential-family form, IRLS and deviance) | Practice derivations of the kind asked in the midterm |
| Sessions 9–11 slides and Lab 03 | — | Interpretation, deviance tests, dispersion, residuals |

## Optional

- Venables and Ripley (2002), *Modern Applied Statistics with S*, 4th ed., Sec. 7.2 (the budworm example and LD50 with `dose.p`).
- Faraway (2016), *Extending the Linear Model with R*, 2nd ed., Ch. 5 (the `esdcomp` and `gala` data appear in the count-regression exercises and examples).

## What to focus on

1. Matching a response to a distribution, link and offset.
2. Reading a coefficient table and a deviance table without software.
3. Knowing when the deviance is a goodness-of-fit statistic (grouped data, large counts) and when not (binary data).
4. Using the Pearson $\hat\phi$ and seeing how overdispersion changes conclusions.
