R examples

Statistical Modelling, Bachelor in Applied Mathematics (BAM), IE University, Spring 2027.

Prof. Dae-Jin Lee, daelee@faculty.ie.edu, IE University — School of Science and Technology.

Each session is one folder, matching the slides. Open the folder in RStudio and knit the .Rmd files, or source the matching .R script. Work from inside the folder so that data/... paths resolve.

Once, before the first knit

install.packages(c("rmarkdown", "knitr", "tidyverse", "MASS", "faraway", "car",
                   "glmnet", "nlme", "lme4", "mgcv", "gamm4", "survival"))

The graded computer labs are in github.com/iedaejin/bam-statmod. Source of this page: github.com/iedaejin/bam-statmod-r-examples.
