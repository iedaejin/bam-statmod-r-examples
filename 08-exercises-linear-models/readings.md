**Faculty:** Prof. Dae-Jin Lee (`daelee@faculty.ie.edu`)

## What to bring

A calculator. No software is used in class (midterm format).

## What to focus on

1. Rebuilding every column of a `summary(lm)` table from $\hat\beta$, standard errors and RSS.
2. Reading interactions: slopes per group, where lines cross, whether that is inside the data.
3. The outlier test with a Bonferroni cut-off, and the difference between leverage, outlyingness and influence.
4. The choice of model for an explanatory vs a predictive question.
