## Part C — Preparation tasks for Session 30

1. **Place your project in the decision table.** In three sentences: response type, data structure, shape of effects, and which doubt your second model class answers.
2. **Build the comparison table** for your report: model, response, number of rows used (`nobs()`), effective degrees of freedom, criterion (AIC, LR test or CV error), and one line on why the comparison is valid.
3. **Write the one-sentence answer** to your research question, with the effect size, its 95% CI and the scope (population, design). This sentence is your last slide in Session 30.
