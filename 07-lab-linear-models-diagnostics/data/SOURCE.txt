savings.csv
  Source: R package 'faraway' (Julian Faraway), version 1.0.9, object 'savings': savings rates in
  50 countries, averaged over 1960-1970. The same data are 'LifeCycleSavings' in R's 'datasets' package.
  Columns: country (row name in the package), sr (personal saving divided by disposable income, %),
  pop15 (% population under 15), pop75 (% population over 75), dpi (per-capita disposable income,
  US dollars), ddpi (% growth rate of dpi).
  Original reference: Belsley, D. A., Kuh, E. and Welsch, R. E. (1980) Regression Diagnostics.
  Wiley. Also used in Faraway, J. J. (2014) Linear Models with R, 2nd ed., CRC Press.
  Values unchanged; written with
  write.csv(data.frame(country = rownames(savings), savings, row.names = NULL), row.names = FALSE).

prostate.csv
  Source: R package 'faraway' (Julian Faraway), version 1.0.9, object 'prostate': 97 men with
  prostate cancer who were due to receive a radical prostatectomy.
  Columns: lcavol (log cancer volume), lweight (log prostate weight), age, lbph (log benign
  prostatic hyperplasia amount), svi (seminal vesicle invasion, 0/1), lcp (log capsular
  penetration), gleason (Gleason score), pgg45 (percentage Gleason scores 4 or 5),
  lpsa (log prostate-specific antigen).
  Source given in the package: Andrews, D. F. and Herzberg, A. M. (1985) Data. Springer-Verlag.
  Original study: Stamey, T. A. et al. (1989) Prostate specific antigen in the diagnosis and
  treatment of adenocarcinoma of the prostate. II. Radical prostatectomy treated patients.
  Journal of Urology 141, 1076-1083.
  Values unchanged; written with write.csv(prostate, row.names = FALSE).
